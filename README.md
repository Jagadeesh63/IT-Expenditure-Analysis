# IT-Expenditure-Analysis

In this presentation, we will explore about IT expenditure Analysis using Power BI Dashboard
 
This project focuses on analyzing IT expenditure across different business areas, regions, and cost categories using Power BI. 
The goal is to create an interactive dashboard that provides transparency into actual vs. forecast vs. planned IT costs. 
The analysis highlights areas of overspending, underutilization, and budget alignment across business units and geographies.

1. Monthly Expenditure Trend

Track Record vs. Plan: Actual spending tracked notably below the planned budget from January through August.
Forecast Divergence: From July onward, the updated forecast projected significantly higher spending than both actuals and original plans.
Year-End Spike: December saw a sharp surge in actual spend, reaching nearly $95M—far surpassing both the monthly plan and forecast.

2. Actual vs. Plan by Business Area

Infrastructure Dominance: Infrastructure represents the largest total investment at over $250M, slightly exceeding its planned budget.
Budget Discipline: Nearly all other business areas came in slightly under plan.

3. Actual Spend Distribution by Cost Element Group

Labor-Heavy Structure: Labor accounts for more than half of all expenditure at 52.6%.
Secondary Drivers: Depreciation & Amortization and Hardware & Software make up the next largest shares, while Shared Services and Other represent minimal costs.

4. Actual Spend by Region

Geographic Concentration: The USA heavily dominates total spending at over $700M.
Minor Regional Footprint: Europe represents the second largest market at ~$110M, while Latin America, Aus & NZ, Canada, and Africa & Asia combine for a negligible fraction of the overall expenditure.
